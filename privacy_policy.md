# Privacy Policy for ChatORAI

**Last updated:** March 2026

ChatORAI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### API Key
- The OpenRouter API key you enter in the app settings is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences.
- **We do not collect, transmit, or store your API key on our servers.**
- The API key is used directly to communicate with OpenRouter's API from your device.

### Chat Messages
- Chat messages are stored locally on your device in SharedPreferences.
- Messages are not transmitted to our servers; they are sent directly to OpenRouter's API for processing.
- You can delete your chat history at any time from within the app.

### Permissions
ChatORAI may request the following permissions:

- **Camera**: To take photos that can be attached to chat messages.
- **Microphone**: For voice input functionality.
- **Photo Library/File Storage**: To select images or files from your device to share in chat.

All permissions are optional and are only used when you explicitly use these features.

## Data Sharing with Third Parties

We use OpenRouter (https://openrouter.ai) as our AI model provider. When you send messages:
- Your message content and any attached media are transmitted directly to OpenRouter's API.
- OpenRouter's privacy policy applies to their processing of your data. Please review their privacy policy at: https://openrouter.ai/privacy

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

## Data Security

- Your API key and chat history are stored locally using Flutter's SharedPreferences.
- We recommend not sharing your API key and using app-specific passwords where available.
- Always use secure networks when transmitting data to external services.

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date.
